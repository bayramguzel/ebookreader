## 🇬🇧 English

**📱 App Name:** eBook Reader
**📝 Short Description:** A powerful and lightweight eBook reader for PDF, EPUB, MOBI and more.
**📚 Full Description:**
Read your favorite books in various formats with eBook Reader. Our app supports PDF, EPUB, FB2, MOBI, AZW3, CBZ, TXT, HTML, and XML. 📖✨

✨ **Features:**

- 🌙 Dark mode support
- 🔠 Customizable font size
- 🗂️ Grid and List view options
- ❤️ Favorite books management
- ✍️ Note-taking for each book
- 🔍 Fast scanning of your device for books
- 💎 User-friendly interface with glassmorphic design
